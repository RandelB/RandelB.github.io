How to Opt In: Sophie Notifications

Sophie is the personal assistant of Randel Bennett (sole proprietor). The program sends call notifications, task-status updates, and action-needed alerts. It is not used for marketing.

Status: This page describes the intended opt-in flow. The flow has not yet been implemented or tested, and the messages below are not records of enrollment that has already occurred. Notifications stay disabled until the flow is completed and tested.

Who can opt in

Messages are sent only to the program owner and, once it has separately completed its own opt-in, the owner's own assistant phone number. Each number must complete the steps below on its own.

How to opt in

Text START to (312) 467-7548.

Intended message exchange

Subscriber sends:
START
Sophie replies (welcome message):
Sophie: Welcome to Sophie notifications for Randel Bennett. You'll get call alerts, task updates and action-needed notices; frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Reply Y to confirm.
Subscriber sends:
Y
Sophie replies (enrollment confirmation):
Sophie: You're enrolled in call and task notifications. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Other keywords

  • HELP, INFO, or SUPPORT: Sophie: For help with notifications, email Sophie_7524@icloud.com. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, END, or QUIT: Sophie: You have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. Reply START to rejoin.

Details

Message frequency varies based on calls and tasks. Message and data rates may apply.
