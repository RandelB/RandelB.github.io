Effective date: October 29, 2026

What this covers

This policy explains how information is handled for the Sophie text message notification program. The program sends call notifications, task-status updates, and action-needed alerts. It is not used for marketing.

Information we collect

  • The mobile phone number that receives notifications.
  • A record of opt-in consent, including the date and time and the keyword used.
  • The content and delivery status of notification messages.
  • Messages sent to the program number, such as START, Y, HELP, and STOP.

How we use it

  • To send call notifications, task-status updates, and action-needed alerts.
  • To keep a record of consent.
  • To respond to HELP and STOP requests.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Messages are delivered through a messaging service provider, Twilio, which processes message data only to deliver the messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
